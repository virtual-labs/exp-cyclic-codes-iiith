### Textbooks

**1. Error Control Coding**<br/>
BY: S. Lin and D. Costello - 2nd Edition, Prentice-Hall, 2004<br/>
_Comprehensive coverage of cyclic codes, shift register implementations, encoding and decoding algorithms, and syndrome computation._

**2. The Theory of Error Correcting Codes**<br/>
BY: F. MacWilliams and N. Sloane - North-Holland Publishing Company, 1977<br/>
_Classic reference on algebraic coding theory covering cyclic codes, polynomial representations, and distance properties._

**3. Algebraic Codes for Data Transmission**<br/>
BY: Richard E. Blahut - Cambridge University Press, 2003<br/>
_Detailed treatment of cyclic codes, generator polynomials, and systematic encoding techniques._

**4. Fundamentals of Error-Correcting Codes**<br/>
BY: W. Huffman and V. Pless - Cambridge University Press, 2003<br/>
_Accessible introduction to cyclic codes with focus on polynomial algebra and shift register implementations._

**5. A First Course in Coding Theory**<br/>
BY: Raymond Hill - Oxford University Press, 1986<br/>
_Clear explanations of cyclic code properties, generator and parity-check polynomials, and decoding methods._
